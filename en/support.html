Why not just one option for everyone? International sanctions mean most mainstream services (Patreon, PayPal, Stripe) can't pay out to Russia, while payments from Russia don't work on most foreign platforms. A crypto wallet sidesteps that with no intermediaries. So there are separate options for Russia and for the rest of the world — each with the lowest fee available in your country.

03 / Questions

Good to know

Does supporting unlock any paid features?

No. TU Overlays has no paid features and no "pro" tier: everything in the app is free for everyone. Support is a thank-you to the author, not a purchase.

Can I cancel a monthly membership?

Yes, at any time, from your Boosty account. Nothing needs doing on the TU Overlays side, and the app keeps working exactly as before.

Which option is best for the author?

A TON transfer loses the least — you only pay the network fee. The difference is small though, so pick whatever is easiest for you: every option reaches the author.

I want to help, but not with money

That helps too: mention the overlays to your team or on stream, share screenshots, and report any bugs you find on GitHub. A clear bug report saves more time than you'd think.
